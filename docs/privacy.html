Math Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Math Dash is a game made by The Ferrules LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Math Dash does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score on your own device so it can show it to you next time. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if we add online leaderboards), we will update this page before the change goes live.
